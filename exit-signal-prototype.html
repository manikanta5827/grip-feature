Exit Signal

A liquidity transparency layer for bond & SDI listings — showing investors how easily they can exit, before they commit, not after they need to.

Prepared by — Manikanta
For — Grip Invest product team
Status — Unsolicited concept, v1
01The problem today
What investors see
Yield, tenure, credit rating — clearly listed at the point of investing.
What's missing
Any signal for how easily this exact bond can be resold before maturity.
Where it surfaces instead
Only after listing on Sell Anytime — when an emergency exit is already underway.

05How the score is built
FactorSignal usedWhy it matters
Resale velocityMedian days from listing to matched sale, trailing 90 daysDirect measure of how fast an investor can actually exit
Buyer depthDistinct buyers who transacted in this instrument, trailing 30 daysMore buyers = less dependence on finding one specific match
Price stabilityAverage resale price vs. face value on completed exitsFlags whether exits require a steep discount to attract a buyer
Fill rate% of Sell Anytime listings that found a buyer within the windowDistinguishes "slow but sure" from "often unsold"
Instrument class baselineHistorical liquidity pattern for similar tenure / ticket size / issuer typeGives new listings a reasonable starting estimate before their own history exists
